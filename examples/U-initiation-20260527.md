## Business Overview

Unity Software is the leading platform to develop, deploy, and grow games and interactive experiences across mobile, PC, console, and extended reality (XR) devices. The company's platform consists of two complementary solution sets:

**Create Solutions** ($621M in FY2025, 34% of revenue): A suite of tools and services used to build, ship, and run real-time 2D and 3D content. This includes the Unity Engine, AI-driven content creation tools (Unity AI), graphics, animation, audio, and cloud services for asset management and multiplayer support. Create Solutions serves developers across gaming and non-gaming industries including automotive, retail, manufacturing, and healthcare.

**Grow Solutions** ($1,228M in FY2025, 66% of revenue): Primarily advertising and monetization products, including the Unity Ad Network powered by Unity Vector (the company's AI-powered personalization engine), mediation platforms, and publishing services (Supersonic). Aura provides on-device app discovery for telecom operators.

Unity's key competitive advantage is its massive creator community and the interoperability between Create and Grow Solutions. Management reports that 70% of mobile games are made with Unity, and the Unity Runtime reaches approximately 3.5 billion monthly active users through Made with Unity applications. The company is currently executing a strategic portfolio reset, exiting non-core businesses (ironSource Ad Network, Supersonic) to focus on high-margin, AI-driven growth.

## Financial Profile

Unity sits at an inflection point. After a painful 2023-2024 restructuring that included a ~25% workforce reduction and the departure of ironSource founders, the company has returned to growth with accelerating strategic revenue and expanding margins. FY2025 total revenue grew only 2% year-over-year to $1.85B as declines in legacy ironSource masked Vector's rapid expansion. However, Q1 2026 strategic revenue surged 35% year-over-year, with Vector revenue up 80%. The Street expects this acceleration to continue, modeling 11% total revenue growth in FY2026 and 19% in FY2027.

## Management Commentary

**Strategic Priorities:** Unity is executing a portfolio reset to focus on its "Strategic Portfolio": the Unity Engine, consumption services, and monetization solutions. The company is sunsetting the ironSource Ad Network (effective April 30, 2026) and divesting Supersonic to eliminate low-margin, commoditized revenue.

**Vector Momentum:** Management is highly bullish on Vector, the AI-powered advertising platform. Vector delivered four consecutive quarters of ~15% sequential growth, with Q1 2026 revenue 80% higher than a year ago. Management expects Vector to reach a $1 billion annual run rate by end of 2026.

**Path to GAAP Profitability:** CFO Jarrod Yahes outlined three drivers: (1) adjusted EBITDA margin expansion (up 800 bps YoY in Q1 to 27%), (2) SBC declining to 15% of revenue (from 33% in 2024), and (3) M&A amortization falling from $117M in Q1 2026 to ~$55M in Q4 2026 and below $25M in FY2027. Unity now targets GAAP net income profitability by Q4 2026.

**AI Product Pipeline:** Unity AI entered public beta in Q1 2026 with 70% user attachment after five days. The Unity commerce platform is on track for launch in Q2 2026 with partners SciPlay and Voodoo. Runtime data integration (sequential behavioral data from the Unity Runtime) is expected to graduate to live production models in Q2 2026.

**Analyst Concerns Addressed:** Management pushed back on competitive fears from Meta's iOS efforts ("no meaningful impact") and Google Genie ("complementary, not duplicative"). They emphasized that Unity's engine-specific context differentiates its AI tools from generic coding agents.

## Market Opportunity

**Company TAM Claim:** Unity does not explicitly quantify a total addressable market in its filings. However, management notes that the global games market continues to expand, with mobile app releases up 60% year-over-year across iOS and Android, and 90% of game developers already using AI in workflows.

**Independent TAM Estimate:** Newzoo estimates the global games market at $188.8 billion in 2025, growing to approximately $195 billion by 2026. This includes console, PC, and mobile gaming. Unity's addressable subset includes game development tools (estimated ~$5B market) and mobile in-app advertising (estimated ~$130B market).

**Implied Market Share:**
- Game Engine/Tools Market: Unity's Create Solutions represent ~12.6% of an estimated $5B game engine TAM
- Mobile Advertising: Unity's Grow Solutions represent ~0.9% of an estimated $130B mobile ad TAM

**TAM Methodology Limitations:** The game engine TAM is inherently difficult to bound because Unity's freemium model (90% of creators pay nothing) means addressable market calculations depend on monetization assumptions. The mobile ad TAM is dominated by Meta and Google, making Unity's true serviceable addressable market a subset of gaming-focused advertising.

## Key Risks

1. **Profitability Uncertainty:** Unity has a history of GAAP losses and may not sustain profitability. The path to Q4 2026 GAAP profitability depends on Vector growth, cost reductions, and M&A amortization roll-off — any of which could disappoint.

2. **Competition from Larger Platforms:** Meta, Google, and Amazon have greater resources and established advertiser relationships. Vector's AI advantage may erode if competitors replicate the technology.

3. **ironSource/SuperSonic Exit Execution:** The strategic exits create near-term revenue headwinds ($50M in Q2, $45M in Q3) and operational complexity. Divestiture timing and cost removal could lag.

4. **AI Disruption Risk:** Generative AI could reduce demand for traditional game engines or enable competitors to offer comparable tools more cheaply. Management acknowledges this risk in the 10-K.

5. **Platform Dependency:** Apple and Google control iOS/Android policies. Changes to privacy rules, app store fees, or advertising restrictions could harm Grow Solutions.

6. **Stock-Based Compensation Dilution:** SBC remains elevated at 20.6% of revenue, diluting shareholders and masking true cash generation.

7. **Convertible Debt Maturity:** $558M of 2026 Notes mature in November 2026. While management intends to repay with cash, this reduces balance sheet flexibility.

## Investment Framework

### Bull / Bear / Base Cases

**Bull Case:** Vector sustains 15% sequential growth through 2026, driven by runtime data integration and product enhancements (Day 28 ROAS, commerce platform). Strategic revenue grows 35%+ for the full year, and adjusted EBITDA margins expand to 30%+. GAAP profitability is achieved in Q4 2026 as M&A amortization falls below $25M annually. The market re-rates Unity toward Roblox's 7.8x EV/Revenue, implying 30%+ upside from current levels. If FY2027 consensus EPS of $1.33 holds, a 25x forward P/E (peer median) would support a $33+ stock price.

**Bear Case:** Vector growth decelerates as the low-hanging fruit of advertiser migration is harvested. Meta or Google intensify competition in gaming ads, pressuring pricing. The ironSource/Supersonic exits create more disruption than expected, and cost savings fail to materialize. Strategic revenue growth slows to 15%, and the Q4 2026 GAAP profitability target is missed. The multiple compresses toward the trough of 4.0x EV/Revenue, implying 30%+ downside.

**Base Case:** Unity executes the strategic transition with moderate success. Vector grows 40-50% year-over-year but sequential growth moderates to high single digits by year-end. Adjusted EBITDA margins reach 28-30%. GAAP profitability is achieved in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027. The stock trades sideways to modestly higher as the market waits for sustained proof of execution, with the forward P/E of 20x (FY2027) providing a floor.

### Key Debates

**1. Is Vector's growth sustainable or are we seeing a one-time advertiser migration?**
- *Bull:* Vector's growth is broad-based across geographies, genres, and platforms. The runtime data integration (launching Q2) provides a durable data moat. Four consecutive quarters of 15% sequential growth demonstrate product-market fit.
- *Bear:* Much of Vector's growth may reflect advertisers shifting budgets from ironSource to Vector within Unity's existing customer base. Once the migration completes, growth could decelerate sharply. The 80% YoY growth rate is unsustainable as comps toughen.
- *Resolution:* Q3-Q4 2026 earnings will show whether Vector can maintain sequential growth as ironSource becomes immaterial.

**2. Is the path to GAAP profitability real or an accounting fiction?**
- *Bull:* Three structural drivers (EBITDA margin expansion, SBC reduction, M&A amortization roll-off) are all independently verifiable. The company generated $404M FCF in FY2025; GAAP profitability is a modest step from here.
- *Bear:* "Adjusted" metrics have historically masked persistent GAAP losses. SBC at 20% of revenue is still egregious. The Q1 2026 GAAP net loss of $348M (68% margin) shows how far the company has to go.
- *Resolution:* Q4 2026 earnings will confirm or invalidate the GAAP profitability target.

**3. Does Unity's game engine moat matter in an AI world?**
- *Bull:* AI makes game development more accessible, expanding Unity's addressable market. The engine's cross-platform deployability and 20 years of optimization create switching costs. Unity AI's project-specific context differentiates it from generic coding agents.
- *Bear:* World models (Google Genie, etc.) could eventually bypass traditional engines. If AI can generate complete games from prompts, the value of Unity's toolset diminishes.
- *Resolution:* Unity AI adoption metrics and developer retention data over the next 12 months will clarify the moat's durability.
